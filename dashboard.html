Methodology & UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)

  • Nutritious Meal Conversion: 1 kg of edible surplus food provides approximately 2 balanced meals (500g average serving).
  • Landfill Methane Prevention: Diverting 1 kg of organic food waste from landfills avoids ~2.5 kg of CO₂ equivalent GHG emissions.
  • Global Alignment: Supports UN SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption), and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

How GATE-WAY Works

An end-to-end transparent workflow from donor post to final delivery

1

Listing Creation

Donors list surplus food with quantity, expiry timer, and encrypted S3 proof photo.

2

Priority Match

NGOs discover nearby food scored by distance, expiry urgency, and capacity.

3

Logistics & Volunteers

NGOs or community volunteers accept and coordinate pickup times.

4

Verified Impact

Upon delivery completion, platform stats & ESG reports update dynamically.
